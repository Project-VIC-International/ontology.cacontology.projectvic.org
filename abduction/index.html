CAC Stranger Abduction & Weapon-Based Coercion Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/abduction#

Definition: Specialized ontology for modeling stranger abduction patterns, weapon-based coercion, disguise use, and opportunistic predation in child sexual exploitation cases, based on cases like Brooklyn DA v. Christopher Fiesco (2024).
