CAC Legal Outcomes & Sentencing Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/legal-outcomes#

Definition: An ontology for representing legal outcomes, federal sentences, judicial proceedings, and punishment guidelines in Internet Crimes Against Children cases with gUFO foundational ontology enhancements for improved semantic precision, temporal modeling, and validation capabilities, extending UCO and CASE.
